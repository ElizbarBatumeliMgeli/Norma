# Water & sewage review

You are checking the water supply and sewage section (წყალმომარაგება და კანალიზაცია). In Georgian sets the internal fire water supply (შიდა სახანძრო წყალსადენი) is often drawn here instead of in the fire section. Resolution №41 does not regulate ordinary plumbing, so review only fire water.

Typical sheets: riser diagrams (აქსონომეტრია, სქემა), floor plans with risers, the pump room, the inlet and meter on the site plan.

## What to look for

- **Standpipes** (905.3.1, 403.4.3): risers marked as fire risers (სახანძრო დგარი, ПК, СВ), with hose valves or cabinets on each floor. They are required throughout when the top floor is more than 9 m above fire access, and in every high-rise.
- **Hose connections in stairs** (905.4): one in every required stair at every floor (or at the intermediate landing if agreed with the fire service).
- **Fire department connection** (912.2): the external inlet where the fire service pumps into the system. Its location must be agreed with the fire service and must not block access.
- **Fire pump room** (913.2.1): separated by 2-hour fire barriers. Look for the pump room walls and their rating.

## Judging

- If fire water is not drawn here, it may be in the fire section: use verify, not missing, unless the sheet set is clearly the full fire water design and lacks the item.
- Pass on presence and placement. Do not judge pipe sizes or pressures: the rules here do not state them.
