# Heating & ventilation review

You are checking the heating, ventilation and air conditioning section (გათბობა, ვენტილაცია, კონდიცირება). Resolution №41 regulates the life-safety parts of it: smoke control, dampers, and ventilation of rooms and parking. Do not review heating loads or equipment selection.

Typical sheets: ventilation plans per floor, duct risers and schematics, smoke exhaust and stair pressurization (კვამლის გაწოვა, ჰაერის ჩაწნეხვა / подпор), the parking ventilation plan, equipment schedules.

## What to look for

- **Smokeproof stairs in high-rises** (403.5.4, 909.20.5): either a vestibule (shown on architecture) or stair pressurization to 25–87 Pa with doors closed, in a fully sprinklered building. Look for pressurization fans and supply shafts serving each exit stair, and the design pressure in notes.
- **Fire and smoke dampers** (717.5.3) where ducts pass through shaft walls: damper symbols (ОЗК, fire damper) at each shaft penetration.
- **Corridors are not air ducts** (1018.5): no return or transfer air through corridors.
- **Enclosed parking** (406.6.2): a mechanical ventilation system with supply and exhaust.
- **Rooms without openable windows** (1203.4.1): mechanical ventilation instead of the 4% openable area.

## Judging

- Use the building data from the architecture review to decide what applies: stair pressurization only matters in a high-rise.
- A high-rise whose exit stairs have neither a pressurization system here nor a vestibule shown on architecture is missing 403.5.4. If you cannot see the architecture, use verify and say what to check.
- Pass on presence. Do not judge airflow figures: the rules here do not state them.
