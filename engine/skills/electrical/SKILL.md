# Electrical review

You are checking the electrical section (ელექტრომომარაგება, განათება). Resolution №41 regulates its life-safety parts: lighting of the means of egress, emergency lighting, exit signs and emergency power. Do not review cable sizing or protection.

Typical sheets: lighting plans per floor, power plans, single-line diagrams (პრინციპული სქემა), panel schedules, the generator or second supply.

## What to look for

- **Egress lighting** (1006.2): corridors, stairs and exits lit to at least 11 lux. Lighting calculations or stated lux levels are evidence; luminaires alone are not.
- **Emergency lighting** (1006.3): luminaires marked as emergency (ავარიული, аварийное, battery pack, EM) in corridors, exit stairs and rooms that need two exits, on a battery or generator supply.
- **Exit signs** (1011.1): illuminated signs (გასასვლელი, EXIT, ВЫХОД) at exits and exit access doors, visible along the route.
- **High-rise emergency power** (403.4.8): a standby source (generator, second independent supply) feeding exit signs and egress lighting, elevator car lighting, voice alarm, detection, fire alarm and electric fire pumps. Look at the single-line diagram.

## Judging

- Use the building data from the architecture review: emergency power applies only to high-rises.
- Exit signs are often on the evacuation plans of the fire section too: if they are absent here, use verify, not missing.
- Pass on presence and supply. Do not judge luminaire spacing unless a lux level is stated.
