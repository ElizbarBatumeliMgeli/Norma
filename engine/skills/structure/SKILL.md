# Structure review

You are checking the structural section (კონსტრუქცია) for what Resolution №41 asks of the structure. Structural design itself (loads, reinforcement, seismic) is regulated elsewhere: do not review it.

Typical sheets: general notes (ზოგადი მითითებები), foundation and slab plans, column and wall schedules, sections, reinforcement details.

## What to look for

- **Construction type** (602.1): the notes may name it (I-A, I-B…). A reinforced concrete frame is usually type I. If it is not stated anywhere, report missing for 602.1, and say what the drawings suggest.
- **Fire resistance of the frame** (table 601: I-A 3 h, I-B 2 h for the primary frame). It may be stated directly, or follow from concrete cover and member size. Report the cover or rating you find. If neither is given, verify.
- **Floors between dwelling units** (420.3) are horizontal assemblies: a concrete slab of normal thickness is one. Report its thickness.
- **Shafts** (713.4): lift and riser shaft walls of 2 h when they connect four or more storeys.
- **Roof platform** over 75 m (916.2): a rescue cabin platform must take at least 25 kN.

## Judging

- Most clauses here are verify unless the notes state a rating or type: structural sets rarely do.
- Do not fail a member on fire resistance unless the drawing states a rating or cover below what the type needs.
- The building data from the architecture review tells you the storeys and height: use it to decide which clauses apply.
