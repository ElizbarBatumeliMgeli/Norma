# Low voltage (ELV) review

You are checking the low-voltage section (სუსტი დენები). In Georgian sets the fire alarm (სახანძრო სიგნალიზაცია) is often drawn here instead of in the fire section. Review only what Resolution №41 asks of it: the fire alarm and its notification. Do not review data, CCTV or access control.

Typical sheets: fire alarm plans per floor, riser diagrams, the panel location, the legend of devices.

## What to look for

- **Manual alarm** (907.2.1 თვ, 907.2.2 სქ, 907.2.9.1 სც-2): manual call points (ხელის მაუწყებელი, ИПР) at exits on every floor, with sounders or notification devices.
- **Automatic smoke detection in high-rises** (907.2.13): smoke detectors (კვამლის დეტექტორი, ИП) throughout, plus the fire service communication system and the emergency voice/alarm system (speakers, a voice evacuation panel).
- The **fire alarm panel** location: in a high-rise it belongs in the fire command centre (403.4.5).

## Judging

- Use the building data from the architecture review to decide which alarm clause applies and whether the building is a high-rise.
- The alarm may be drawn in the fire section instead: if it is absent here, use verify, not missing.
- Pass on presence and coverage of every floor. Do not judge detector spacing: the rules here do not state it.
